❓ Glossary & how this list is built
FBAR
A FinCEN report (not a tax form) that US persons must file if the combined balance of their non-US financial accounts exceeded $10,000 at any point in the year.
FATCA
Foreign Account Tax Compliance Act — requires foreign banks to report US-person account holders to the IRS, and requires those account holders to file Form 8938 above certain thresholds.
Exit tax (US)
The US's own departure tax (IRC Section 877A) for certain long-term Green Card holders or citizens who expatriate — can deem worldwide assets sold on the way out.
