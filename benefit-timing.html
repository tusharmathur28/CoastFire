❓ Glossary & how this is calculated
Totalization
The Canada-US Social Security Agreement — lets you combine CPP/QPP and US Social Security credits to qualify for benefits you would not qualify for on either country's work history alone.

How do the early/late adjustments work? CPP shrinks about 0.6%/month claimed before 65 and grows about 0.7%/month claimed after, OAS grows about 0.6%/month delayed past 65, and Social Security shrinks up to 5/9% per month for the first 36 months before your full retirement age (67) and 5/12% per month beyond that, then grows about 2/3%/month delayed past 67 — all standard government formulas, not estimates.
