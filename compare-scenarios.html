❓ How this comparison works

Each saved scenario is re-run through the same CoastFIRE projection engine as the calculator itself, using exactly the inputs it had when you saved it — including its own exchange rate and reporting currency. Changing your current inputs on the calculator afterward doesn't affect a saved scenario; you'd need to save it again under the same name to update it.
