Living cross-border complicates your financial picture. Your accounts sit in two currencies, under two tax systems, and not every wrapper is treated the same way by both governments. These tools keep your Canadian and US accounts separate until the very last step, converting only the totals, and flag the account-level quirks that matter for someone in your position.

These tools are not tax, legal, or immigration advice. They don't model cross-border taxation — treat every dollar figure as a pre-tax planning target. Cross-border tax rules (RRSP/TFSA treatment, PFIC, FBAR/FATCA, departure tax, totalization) are genuinely complex and change; work with a cross-border-specialist accountant or financial planner before acting on this.
